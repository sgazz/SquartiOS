## Full App Store Description
Squart is a minimalist strategy board game built around one idea: control space before it disappears.

Two players place two-cell pieces with different orientation rules:
- Horizontal places left-to-right
- Vertical places top-to-bottom

Every move removes options. You win by leaving your opponent without a legal placement.

### Features
- Quiet tactical gameplay focused on blocking and position
- Two board modes:
  - 2D Board for clean, immediate readability
  - 3D Board for spatial overview and cinematic presentation
- Local Player vs Player
- Player vs AI with three difficulties:
  - Easy
  - Medium
  - Hard
- Daily Challenge with deterministic daily setups
- Multiple board shapes and board sizes
- Refined touch controls, previews, and camera tools
